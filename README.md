Namma Kathey is a digital storytelling application designed to provide users with an engaging platform to read, write, and share stories. The app encourages creativity by allowing users to publish their own stories, explore different genres, and connect with readers through an interactive environment. It aims to promote storytelling culture and provide a space for writers to showcase their talent.

🚀 Features
👤 User Management
User Registration and Login
Secure Authentication
Profile Management
📚 Story Reading
Read stories from different categories
Personalized recommendations
Save favorite stories
✍️ Story Writing
Upload and publish stories
Edit and manage content
Add story descriptions and genres
❤️ User Interaction
Like and comment on stories
Share feedback with writers
Follow favorite authors
🔍 Search & Categories
Search stories by title or author
Browse stories based on genres
